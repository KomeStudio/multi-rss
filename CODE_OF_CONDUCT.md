# Kome Studio Code of Conduct

**Building a welcoming, creative, and respectful community.**

At Kome Studio, we believe creativity thrives when people feel safe, respected, and welcome. This Code of Conduct outlines the standards we expect from everyone participating in our projects, games, websites, and online communities.

Our goal is to create a positive environment where players, developers, artists, contributors, and community members can collaborate, share ideas, and enjoy our work together.

Read Kome Studio’s Code of Conduct for respectful collaboration, safe gaming, responsible contributions, and a welcoming community across our projects.

## 1. Our Commitment

We are committed to providing an inclusive and respectful environment for everyone, regardless of personal background, identity, experience, abilities, language, beliefs, or circumstances.

Everyone who participates in good faith is entitled to fair treatment, dignity, and respect. We expect all community members to contribute responsibly and help maintain a welcoming environment.

## 2. Expected Behavior

All participants in Kome Studio communities and projects are expected to:

1. **Be respectful.** Treat others with courtesy and consideration.
2. **Communicate constructively.** Share feedback, criticism, and disagreements without personal attacks.
3. **Welcome different perspectives.** Respect diverse experiences, ideas, and creative approaches.
4. **Act responsibly.** Take ownership of your words, actions, and contributions.
5. **Respect boundaries.** Stop unwanted interactions when someone asks you to do so.
6. **Protect privacy.** Respect personal information and confidential communications.
7. **Support the community.** Help create a friendly, safe, and collaborative environment.

We recognize that misunderstandings can happen. When they do, we encourage good-faith communication, accountability, and reasonable efforts to resolve the issue.

## 3. Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not permitted in Kome Studio communities, projects, or activities:

- **Harassment:** Unwanted personal attention, intimidation, threats, or repeated contact after a clear request to stop.
- **Personal attacks:** Insults, humiliation, abusive language, or deliberately demeaning comments directed at individuals or groups.
- **Discrimination:** Unfair treatment, hateful conduct, or stereotyping based on personal characteristics or identity.
- **Threats and violence:** Encouraging, threatening, or promoting harm against individuals or groups.
- **Privacy violations:** Publishing private information, private conversations, or confidential material without appropriate permission.
- **Impersonation:** Misrepresenting your identity or pretending to represent Kome Studio without authorization.
- **Plagiarism and misattribution:** Presenting another person's work as your own or failing to provide appropriate credit where required.
- **Spam and disruptive promotion:** Repeated unsolicited advertising, scams, or commercial promotion that disrupts community activities.
- **Abuse of services:** Using our websites, games, or community channels to harass others, distribute malicious content, or disrupt legitimate participation.
- **Retaliation:** Threatening or punishing anyone for making a good-faith report or participating in an investigation.

These standards apply to behavior that undermines the safety, trust, or well-being of our community.

## 4. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to Kome Studio's official websites, games, repositories, communication channels, and community spaces, including:

- **Kome Studio:** [https://komestudio.com](https://komestudio.com)
- **Kome Top:** [https://kome.top](https://kome.top)
- Official Kome Studio projects, repositories, and associated online communities.

It also applies when someone is officially representing Kome Studio in public, at events, or through an official account or communication channel.

Third-party platforms may have their own terms, policies, and moderation rules, which continue to apply.

## 5. Reporting a Concern

If you experience or witness behavior that may violate this Code of Conduct, please contact the Kome Studio team through an official contact channel listed on [komestudio.com](https://komestudio.com).

When reporting an incident, include relevant details such as what happened, when it occurred, where it happened, and any supporting information you can safely provide.

Please do not publish another person's private information when reporting a concern.

Kome Studio will review reports in good faith and take reasonable steps to protect the privacy and safety of those involved. We will handle reports as discreetly as reasonably possible, recognizing that complete confidentiality cannot always be guaranteed.

We do not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern honestly or participates in a review.

## 6. Enforcement

Depending on the nature, severity, and context of a violation, Kome Studio may take one or more of the following actions:

1. **Warning:** A private notice explaining the concern and the expected change in behavior.
2. **Restricted Participation:** Temporary limits on specific interactions, features, or community activities.
3. **Temporary Suspension:** Temporary removal of access to relevant community spaces, services, or participation privileges.
4. **Permanent Ban or Access Removal:** Permanent removal from relevant community spaces or services for serious or repeated violations.

Kome Studio may skip intermediate steps when the severity of an incident warrants stronger action. Enforcement decisions will consider the available information, the impact on affected people, relevant platform policies, and the need to protect the community.

Where appropriate, we may also remove content that violates this Code of Conduct.

## 7. Resolving Conflict and Repairing Harm

Not every disagreement is a violation. Differences of opinion, constructive criticism, and respectful debate are welcome.

When harm occurs, we encourage appropriate accountability and reasonable efforts to address its impact. Depending on the circumstances, this may include an apology, correction of harmful content, or other steps that help restore trust.

Resolution efforts do not replace necessary protective measures, and affected individuals are not required to engage directly with someone who has harmed them.

## 8. Community Responsibilities

Everyone participating in Kome Studio projects shares responsibility for maintaining a respectful environment.

Community moderators and project maintainers are expected to apply this Code of Conduct fairly and consistently within their authority. Community members are encouraged to raise concerns, respect moderation decisions, and help keep discussions constructive.

Nothing in this Code of Conduct overrides applicable law or the terms and policies of the platforms on which our projects operate.

## 9. Updates to This Code

Kome Studio may update this Code of Conduct as our projects, services, and communities evolve.

The current version published in the relevant official repository or on an official Kome Studio website will serve as the reference for community expectations.
